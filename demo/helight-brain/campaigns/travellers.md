# Campaign -- Frequent Travellers

## Hypothesis

Frequent travellers dealing with jet lag and unfamiliar rooms will respond to a portable, no-setup routine anchor more than to a general sleep-quality pitch.

## Formats

Unboxing and first-night format, shot in a hotel-room setting rather than a home bedroom, to make the portability case visually rather than only in copy.

## Status
