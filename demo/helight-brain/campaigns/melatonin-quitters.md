# Campaign -- Quitting Melatonin

## Hypothesis

A drug-free alternative resonates most strongly with someone actively trying to reduce reliance on melatonin or another sleep aid, making this the strongest fit for the drug-free angle in [[wiki/angle-bank]].

## Formats

Comparison-against-melatonin-and-sleep-apps format, citing [[sources/sleep-onset-2018]] directly.

## Status

Live, sixth week of testing, the longest-running of the six lane tests.

## Illustrative numbers

Click-through rate at 1.8%, the highest of the six active lanes; cost per purchase at $39.

Seeded for the demo: illustrative, not a measured figure.

Related lane: [[wiki/audience-lanes]]. See [[wiki/format-playbook]] and [[writing/hook-vault]] for the underlying assets this campaign draws on.

Creative for this lane leans on [[people/dr-pedram-navab]]'s public commentary
where a physician voice strengthens the drug-free claim, and every comparison
line against melatonin itself, not against a named competitor brand, stays
within the "never" rules in [[wiki/claims-allowlist]].
