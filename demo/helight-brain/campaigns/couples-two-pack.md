# Campaign -- Couples -- Two-Pack

## Hypothesis

Couples buying for two nightstands are a higher-order-value segment than a single-unit buyer, and gifting occasions (moving in together, anniversaries) are a stronger conversion trigger than a general sleep pitch.

## Formats

Gifting-occasion hooks from [[writing/hook-vault]], paired with the wind-down routine format shown across two nightstands, per [[products/sleep-two-pack]]'s per-side positioning.

## Status

Live, second week of testing, TikTok only.

## Illustrative numbers

Average order value $263 (roughly two-pack price point plus a small attach rate); cost per purchase at $58, higher than the account average due to the smaller, newer lane.

Seeded for the demo: illustrative, not a measured figure.
