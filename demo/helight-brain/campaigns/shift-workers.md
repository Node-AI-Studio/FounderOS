# Campaign -- Shift Workers and Nurses

## Hypothesis

Shift workers face a circadian-rhythm problem that most general sleep content ignores, and a science-forward, mechanism-first message will outperform a lifestyle-forward one with this audience.

## Formats

Physician explainer format, likely voiced by [[people/dr-ed-omalley]], plus the sleep-inertia angle from [[sources/sleep-inertia-2019]] since waking quality after an irregular schedule matters more here than sleep onset alone.
