# Campaign -- Parents of Bad Sleepers -- Kidzzz

## Hypothesis

Parents already managing a child's difficult bedtime routine will respond to a drug-free, no-wearable device more readily than another supplement or app, provided the safety framing stays within [[decisions/no-invented-product-specs]] and [[wiki/claims-allowlist]].

## Formats

Parent POV format (see [[wiki/format-playbook]]), using hook templates from [[writing/hook-vault]]'s parent-facing set. Primary product [[products/helight-kidzzz]].

## Status

Live, in its fourth week of testing across Meta and TikTok.

## Illustrative numbers

Week 1 to 4 click-through rate trending from 1.1% to 1.6%; cost per purchase trending down from $54 to $41.

Seeded for the demo: illustrative, not a measured figure.
