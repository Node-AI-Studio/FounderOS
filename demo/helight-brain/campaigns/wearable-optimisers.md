# Campaign -- Wearable Optimisers (Oura, Whoop)

## Hypothesis

Buyers already tracking sleep on a wearable are primed to trust a device that can point to independent research, making the study chain and patent angles more persuasive here than the guarantee or price.

## Formats

Wearable before-and-after format, pairing an Oura or Whoop sleep score screenshot with a Helight usage period, citing [[sources/basketball-players-2012]] for the performance angle.

## Status

Live, third week of testing, Meta only.

## Illustrative numbers

Click-through rate steady around 1.4%; cost per purchase around $47, roughly in line with the account average.

Seeded for the demo: illustrative, not a measured figure.
