# SMS Voice

Voice guide for SMS sends, shorter and more direct than
[[writing/email-voice]], reserved for time-sensitive or high-intent moments:
shipping confirmation, the night-1 setup reminder from
[[sops/21-nights-coach]], and abandoned-cart recovery.

No claim beyond what fits in a single short message should attempt the full
nuance of [[wiki/claims-allowlist]]; instead, SMS points back to email or
the site for anything requiring more than one line of qualification. A results
or guarantee claim that cannot be stated accurately in SMS length should not
be forced into SMS length.

Frequency is capped deliberately low relative to email, since SMS carries a
higher annoyance cost per message. [[people/retention-lead]] reviews SMS
send frequency alongside flow performance in
[[sops/lifecycle-planner]]'s monthly review, not as a separate cadence
decision.
