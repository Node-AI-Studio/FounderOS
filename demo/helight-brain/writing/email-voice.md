# Email Voice

Voice guide for every Klaviyo-sent email, owned jointly by
[[people/retention-lead]] and [[sops/lifecycle-planner]]. Warmer and more
personal than paid ad copy, written as if from one person to a specific
buyer rather than a brand-to-audience broadcast, since the retention
relationship is one-to-one by nature.

Subject lines avoid urgency tactics not backed by a real constraint (no fake
countdown timers, no invented scarcity), consistent with
[[decisions/no-invented-product-specs]]'s broader honesty standard. The
21-nights coaching sequence from [[sops/21-nights-coach]] in particular
should read as encouragement, not a sales push, since its job is adherence
to the routine, not a second purchase.

Every email referencing the guarantee, results timing or the patent still
draws its exact language from [[wiki/claims-allowlist]], even though the
tone around that language is warmer than an ad. See
[[projects/seven-klaviyo-flows]] for which flows currently need voice review.
