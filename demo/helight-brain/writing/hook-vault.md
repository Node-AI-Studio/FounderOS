# Hook Vault

Thirty slotted hook templates, maintained by [[sops/hook-miner]] and pulled
from by [[sops/content-planner]] and [[sops/ad-copywriter]] for every new
piece of content. Bracketed terms are slots to fill per campaign; every
filled slot still needs to clear [[wiki/claims-allowlist]] before use.

Categories map to [[wiki/audience-lanes]] and [[wiki/angle-bank]]; a hook
tagged "21-nights framing" pairs naturally with
[[wiki/the-21-nights-series]], and a hook tagged "Guarantee" pairs with
[[decisions/guarantee-as-risk-reversal]]. [[sops/hook-miner]] retires any
entry that stops converting and adds new candidates weekly rather than
letting this list go stale.
