# Decision -- Claims allowlist governs every outbound claim

**Date:** September 2026

## Context

Helight sells a device with a physician-endorsed, research-backed mechanism into a category (sleep, health) with real regulatory and platform-policy exposure. A single ungated claim can trigger an ad account suspension or worse, and the [[wiki/the-quebec-broadcast]] history shows what an unfounded-science narrative can do to the whole business.

## Decision

No claim about Helight's mechanism, results or safety may publish anywhere (paid, organic or on-site) without clearing [[wiki/claims-allowlist]] first. The allowlist, not an individual writer's judgment, is the source of truth for what can be said.

## Consequences

Every content and copy workflow gains a mandatory gate step. This slows output slightly but removes the single largest platform and reputational risk the brand carries.

Governs [[sops/compliance-auditor]].
