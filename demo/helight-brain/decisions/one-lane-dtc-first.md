# Decision -- One lane, DTC first

**Date:** September 2026

## Context

Helight sells DTC, wholesale, and now two retail placements. Splitting attention evenly across all of these risks under-resourcing the channel that actually produces most of current revenue.

## Decision

DTC on the Shopify storefront (EN, FR, DE, plus the Canadian store) is the primary lane. Wholesale and retail get status tracking and support, not equal build priority, until DTC's core systems (proof pages, the seven Klaviyo flows, paid amplification) are fully live.

## Consequences

Wholesale and retail partners may feel under-served in the short term. DTC's compounding channels (site, email, paid) get built out to full strength first, which is the higher-leverage sequencing for a company this size.
