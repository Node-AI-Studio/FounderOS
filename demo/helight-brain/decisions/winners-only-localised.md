# Decision -- Winners only get localised

**Date:** September 2026

## Context

Translating every piece of content into FR and DE (per the storefront's locale set) is expensive and slow, and most content never earns its translation cost back.

## Decision

Only content already proven to be a winner in the primary market gets translated and localised for FR and DE, rather than translating on a fixed publishing schedule.

## Consequences

FR and DE audiences see content with a short delay relative to the primary market, since a piece has to prove itself first. That delay is accepted in exchange for not spending translation budget on content that would have underperformed anyway.

Governs [[sops/growth-planner]].

[[people/head-of-growth]] is the checkpoint for what counts as proven, using
the same weekly data [[sops/budget-auditor]] already reports, so a lane does
not need a separate localisation-readiness review to clear this bar.
