# Decision -- No invented product specs

**Date:** September 2026

## Context

The public brief for Helight's products states duration, wavelength and price, and nothing else. There is real temptation, especially in ad copy and comparison content, to fill in specifics (battery life, brightness, dimensions) that make a product sound more concrete.

## Decision

No page, ad or script may state a product spec that is not confirmed in the public product record. If a spec is unknown, the content omits it rather than inventing a plausible-sounding number.

## Consequences

Some copy reads slightly less concrete than a competitor's spec sheet. In exchange, nothing the brand publishes can be caught out as fabricated if a customer or journalist checks it against the real product.
