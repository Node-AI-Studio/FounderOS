# Decision -- The guarantee is risk reversal, not a cost centre

**Date:** September 2026

## Context

The 60-day money-back guarantee generates real refund volume, and it would be easy for Finance to treat that volume purely as a cost to minimise, which would pressure Customer Care toward denying borderline requests.

## Decision

The guarantee is treated as a deliberate risk-reversal offer element, priced into the business, not a leak to be plugged. [[sops/refund-handler]] processes eligible requests without friction, and [[sops/offer-designer]] leads with the guarantee in copy rather than downplaying it.

## Consequences

Refund rate is tracked honestly in [[sops/contribution-margin]] as a real cost of the offer, and any push to tighten guarantee terms has to clear that margin view first, not a Customer Care team quietly getting stricter.

Governs [[sops/offer-designer]].
