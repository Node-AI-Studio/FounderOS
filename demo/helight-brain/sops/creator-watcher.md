# SOP -- Creator Watcher

## Purpose

Track creator-produced content performance, both Helight's own creator partners and adjacent creators in the sleep and wellness space, to surface winning formats and angles early.

## Trigger

Runs continuously, with a weekly digest delivered to [[sops/content-planner]] every Saturday.

## Steps

1. Pull performance data for all active creator content across platforms.
2. Tag each piece by format from [[wiki/format-playbook]] and angle from [[wiki/angle-bank]].
3. Identify outlier performers, positive or negative, relative to the creator's own baseline.
4. Note any recurring objection or question in comments and route it to [[sops/voice-of-customer]].
5. Compile a weekly digest of winning format-angle pairings.
6. Flag any creator content whose claims may not clear [[wiki/claims-allowlist]] to [[sops/compliance-auditor]].

## Definition of done

A weekly digest of creator performance and flagged compliance concerns reaches [[sops/content-planner]] before the Sunday planning cycle.

## Escalation

A creator repeatedly posting non-compliant claims escalates to [[people/content-lead]] for a direct conversation, not a quiet content pull.

Reports to [[people/content-lead]]. See also [[wiki/angle-bank]].
