# SOP -- 21 Nights Coach

## Purpose

Run the post-purchase email and SMS sequence that coaches a new buyer through their first 21 nights, so the results promise has a real chance to land rather than the device sitting in a drawer after night two.

## Trigger

Triggers automatically on every [[products/helight-sleep]] or [[products/helight-kidzzz]] purchase confirmation.

## Steps

1. Send a night-1 setup reminder within a few hours of delivery confirmation.
2. Send a night-7 check-in asking about early impressions, with a link to common troubleshooting.
3. Send a night-14 encouragement message reinforcing consistent nightly use.
4. Send a night-21 result check-in, routing a positive result toward a review request and a null result toward [[sops/support-triage]].
5. Confirm all messaging carries current, cleared language from [[wiki/claims-allowlist]].
6. Report aggregate 21-night completion and result rates to [[people/retention-lead]] monthly.

## Definition of done

Every purchaser receives the full four-touch sequence and a night-21 result is captured for the majority of completers.

## Escalation

A pattern of null results at night 21 escalates to [[people/retention-lead]] and [[sops/voice-of-customer]] rather than being treated as an isolated case.

Reports to [[people/retention-lead]]. See also [[wiki/the-21-nights-series]].
