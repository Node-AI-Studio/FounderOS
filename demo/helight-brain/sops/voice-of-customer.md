# SOP -- Voice of Customer

## Purpose

Synthesise every week's support tickets, reviews and comments into a weekly report of top objections and questions, with counts and a representative verbatim, and note what changed since last week.

## Trigger

Runs every Friday, compiling the week's data into a report published under customers/.

## Steps

1. Pull all tagged tickets, refund reasons and comment-level flags from the week.
2. Group into recurring objection and question categories with counts.
3. Select one representative verbatim per top category, clearly marked as illustrative rather than a specific real customer transcript.
4. Compare this week's top categories against last week's report and note what changed.
5. Route any category tracing to a missing proof point (for example, questions the empty science page would answer) to [[people/store-manager]].
6. Publish the finished report and notify [[people/retention-lead]] and [[people/head-of-growth]].

## Definition of done

A weekly report exists comparing this week to last, with counts, one representative quote per category, and clear routing of any proof-page gaps.

## Escalation

A sharp week-over-week spike in a single objection escalates same-day to the relevant pillar lead rather than waiting for the Friday report.

Reports to [[people/customer-care-lead]]. See also [[wiki/credibility-gaps-on-the-site]].
