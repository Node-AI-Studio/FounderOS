# SOP -- 21 Nights Producer

## Purpose

Produce the ongoing day 1 / day 7 / day 21 documentary series that follows a real buyer's first three weeks with Helight, per [[wiki/the-21-nights-series]].

## Trigger

Runs on a rolling basis, since a new participant enters the series roughly every few weeks and each one needs three separate touchpoints over 21 days.

## Steps

1. Confirm a participant and product SKU with [[people/content-lead]] before day 1 filming.
2. Capture day 1: unboxing and first-night reaction.
3. Capture day 7: early impression, any change noticed so far.
4. Capture day 21: final result against the 21-nights-or-less promise, honestly, including a null result if that is what happened.
5. Route all three cuts through a human editor per [[decisions/editing-stays-human]] before any cut is considered final.
6. Submit the finished series to [[sops/compliance-auditor]] before scheduling.

## Definition of done

All three touchpoints exist for a given participant, edited and compliance-cleared, before the series is scheduled for publish.

## Escalation

A day 21 result that does not support the results promise escalates to [[people/content-lead]] for a decision on whether and how to publish it honestly, not a decision to discard it.

Reports to [[people/content-lead]]. See also [[wiki/the-21-nights-series]].
