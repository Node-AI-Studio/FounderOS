# SOP -- Knowledge Agent

## Purpose

Keep this brain current: file new sources, cross-link new decisions to the sops they govern, and flag pages that have gone stale.

## Trigger

Runs weekly, and immediately whenever a new decision, source or sop is added anywhere in this brain.

## Steps

1. Check for any new page added since the last run.
2. Confirm new sources under sources/ are linked from [[wiki/evidence-base]] or [[wiki/study-chain]] as appropriate.
3. Confirm new decisions are linked from the sop or pillar they govern.
4. Check existing pages for wikilinks pointing to a page that does not exist, and flag for correction.
5. Flag any page whose status suggests it has not been reviewed in the expected cycle.
6. Report a short weekly summary of what was added, linked or flagged to [[people/operations-assistant]].

## Definition of done

No orphaned page and no broken wikilink exists at the end of a run; every new addition is linked from at least one other relevant page.

## Escalation

A structural gap (a decision with nothing governing it, a pillar with no sop) escalates to [[people/operations-assistant]] for a decision on whether to fill it.

Reports to [[people/operations-assistant]]. See also [[sops/evidence-curator]].
