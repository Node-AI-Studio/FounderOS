# SOP -- Growth Planner

## Purpose

Set the coming week's paid media plan across Meta, TikTok and Google: which audience lanes get budget, at what split, and which creative slots need filling before the week starts.

## Trigger

Runs every Sunday evening ahead of Monday's [[sops/approve-the-week]] review, and re-runs mid-week if [[sops/budget-auditor]] flags a lane pacing far off plan.

## Steps

1. Pull last week's performance by lane from Meta, TikTok and Google.
2. Cross-reference against [[wiki/audience-lanes]] for lanes not yet tested or due for a refresh.
3. Draft a proposed budget split across lanes and platforms, weighted toward last week's winners.
4. Flag any lane needing new creative and route the request to [[sops/content-planner]].
5. Submit the draft plan to [[people/head-of-growth]] for review before Monday.
6. Log the approved plan so [[sops/budget-auditor]] has a baseline to check pacing against.

## Definition of done

A written weekly plan exists with a lane-by-lane budget split, approved by the Head of Growth, and logged before Monday spend begins.

## Escalation

If two lanes tie on performance and budget cannot cover both, escalate the tie to [[people/head-of-growth]] rather than splitting evenly by default.

Reports to [[people/head-of-growth]]. See also [[wiki/audience-lanes]].
