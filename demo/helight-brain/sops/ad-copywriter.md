# SOP -- Ad Copywriter

## Purpose

Turn an approved angle and format into ad copy variants ready for [[sops/compliance-auditor]] review, across headline, primary text and description fields per platform.

## Trigger

Runs whenever [[sops/growth-planner]] assigns a lane a new or refreshed creative slot.

## Steps

1. Pull the assigned angle from [[wiki/angle-bank]] and the assigned format from [[wiki/format-playbook]].
2. Draft 3 to 5 copy variants per platform, varying hook and proof point, never the underlying claim.
3. Check every claim against [[wiki/claims-allowlist]] before drafting is considered finished.
4. Submit variants to [[sops/compliance-auditor]] for gate review.
5. Revise any variant the Compliance Auditor flags rather than arguing the finding.
6. Hand cleared variants to [[sops/publisher]] with the approved lane and format tagged.

## Definition of done

All submitted variants have either cleared Compliance Auditor review or been revised and resubmitted; nothing ships un-gated.

## Escalation

If a strong-performing historical line no longer clears the allowlist, escalate to [[people/head-of-growth]] rather than reusing it unreviewed.

Reports to [[people/head-of-growth]]. See also [[wiki/angle-bank]].
