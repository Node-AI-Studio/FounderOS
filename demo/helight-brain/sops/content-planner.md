# SOP -- Content Planner

## Purpose

Build the week's organic content calendar across formats and audience lanes, sequencing what publishes when and on which platform.

## Trigger

Runs every Sunday alongside [[sops/growth-planner]], since the two calendars have to be read together for the weekly loop to work.

## Steps

1. Review [[sops/creator-watcher]] and [[sops/hook-miner]] output from the prior week.
2. Map open slots against [[wiki/audience-lanes]] and [[wiki/format-playbook]] to avoid repeating the same lane-format pairing two weeks running.
3. Assign each slot a hook from [[writing/hook-vault]].
4. Route scripting for assigned slots to [[writing/scripts-week-37]] or the relevant week's script file.
5. Confirm every slot has a named format and lane before the calendar is considered final.
6. Hand the finished calendar to [[sops/publisher]] once each piece clears [[sops/compliance-auditor]].

## Definition of done

A full week's calendar exists with every slot mapped to a lane, a format and a hook, before Monday.

## Escalation

If two lanes are under-served two weeks running, escalate to [[people/content-lead]] rather than letting the gap persist a third week.

Reports to [[people/content-lead]]. See also [[wiki/format-playbook]].
