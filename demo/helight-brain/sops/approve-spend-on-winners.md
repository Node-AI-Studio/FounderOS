# SOP -- Approve Spend on Winners

## Purpose

Yannick Kiefer's specific approval gate for scaling budget on any lane or angle that [[sops/budget-auditor]] and [[sops/contribution-margin]] have flagged as a genuine winner.

## Trigger

Triggers whenever [[people/head-of-growth]] proposes a spend increase beyond the plan approved in [[sops/approve-the-week]].

## Steps

1. Confirm the proposed lane has a current contribution margin view from [[sops/contribution-margin]], not just a click-through or revenue number.
2. Confirm the lane's compliance status is clean per [[sops/compliance-auditor]] before scaling its reach.
3. Review the proposed new spend level and expected marginal return from [[sops/budget-auditor]].
4. Approve, reduce, or hold the proposed increase.
5. Log the decision and the reasoning, especially any hold, so the same case is not re-argued from scratch the following week.
6. Set a review date for the scaled lane rather than approving it as a standing increase indefinitely.

## Definition of done

Every spend increase beyond the standing weekly plan has an explicit, logged approval tied to a margin view and a review date.

## Escalation

A pillar lead scaling spend without this approval is treated as a process failure to fix immediately, not a result to praise regardless of performance.

Reports to [[people/head-of-growth]]. See also [[sops/contribution-margin]].
