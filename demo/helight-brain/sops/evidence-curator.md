# SOP -- Evidence Curator

## Purpose

Keep every on-site science and evidence reference accurate and sourced against [[wiki/evidence-base]], and lead the rebuild of the currently-empty science page.

## Trigger

Runs whenever a new source is added to sources/, and as the lead agent on [[projects/fill-the-proof-pages]]'s science-page workstream.

## Steps

1. Review any new entry in sources/ for relevance to on-site claims.
2. Cross-check every existing on-site science reference against [[wiki/study-chain]] for accuracy.
3. Draft or update the science page content, citing specific sources rather than general claims.
4. Submit drafted content to [[sops/compliance-auditor]] before publishing.
5. Coordinate with [[people/store-manager]] on page layout and publish timing.
6. Flag any claim already live on-site that does not trace to a real source for immediate correction.

## Definition of done

Every science-related claim on the storefront traces to a specific, real source, and the science page reflects [[wiki/evidence-base]] accurately.

## Escalation

A live, uncleared claim discovered on-site escalates immediately to [[people/store-manager]] for takedown or correction, not scheduled for the next content cycle.

Reports to [[people/store-manager]]. See also [[wiki/evidence-base]].
