# SOP -- Approve the Week

## Purpose

Yannick Kiefer's weekly review of every pillar's plan, numbers and open issues, and the single point where the week's direction is actually set.

## Trigger

Runs every Monday, after [[sops/growth-planner]], [[sops/content-planner]] and [[sops/store-auditor]] have each filed their weekly input.

## Steps

1. Review the proposed Growth plan and budget split from [[sops/growth-planner]].
2. Review the Content calendar from [[sops/content-planner]].
3. Review store health and any open issue from [[sops/store-auditor]].
4. Review the prior week's contribution margin view from [[sops/contribution-margin]].
5. Approve, cut or send back any item that does not clear on margin, compliance or capacity grounds.
6. Record the approved week and any explicitly cut item so no pillar quietly re-proposes a cut item unchanged.

## Definition of done

Every pillar has an approved plan for the week, logged with what was cut and why, before Monday's spend and publishing begins.

## Escalation

A disagreement between two pillar leads over a shared resource (budget, a creative slot) is decided here, not left unresolved into the week.

Reports to [[people/head-of-growth]]. See also [[sops/growth-planner]].
