# The 28-Minute Protocol

The mechanism shared by [[products/helight-sleep]] and
[[products/helight-kidzzz]]: 630 nm red light delivered for 14 minutes,
followed by a 14-minute fade to darkness, 28 minutes total, used at the start
of a sleep routine to mimic a natural sunset.

The scientific basis traces back to Berson and Hattar's 2002 discovery of
intrinsically photosensitive retinal ganglion cells (ipRGCs), the
photoreceptors that signal light and dark to the circadian system independent
of vision. See [[sources/berson-hattar-2002]] and the fuller chain in
[[wiki/study-chain]], which adds a 2012 study on basketball players, a 2017
intensity study, a 2018 sleep-onset study, a 2019 sleep-inertia study and a
2024 Strasbourg study.

The protocol, its 14-plus-14 structure and its light-fade delivery method are
the subject of the company's granted patent family (see
[[wiki/patent-family]]): US 11672943 B2, EP 3519030 A1, JP 7277925 B2, CN
109982736 A, FR 3056408 A1, ES 2928405 T3 and WO 2018060616 A1.

No spec beyond duration, wavelength and the two-phase structure should be
claimed anywhere in copy; see [[decisions/no-invented-product-specs]].
