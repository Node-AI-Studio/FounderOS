# Helight Sleep

$139. The flagship SKU and the product the brand is named for. Delivers
[[products/the-28-minute-protocol]]: 630 nm red light for 14 minutes, then a
14-minute fade to dark, 28 minutes total, used bedside at the start of the
sleep routine.

Launched in Quebec in October 2020, roughly 200,000 units have sold across the
Helight line since. Carries the brand's full offer stack: a "21 nights or
less" results promise (see [[wiki/the-21-nights-series]]), a 60-day
money-back guarantee, and a two-year warranty. The mechanism is covered by a
granted patent family, [[wiki/patent-family]].

Helight Sleep is the anchor SKU in every audience lane in
[[wiki/audience-lanes]] except Kidzzz-specific parents, and it is the product
shown in most format types in [[wiki/format-playbook]]: wearable before/after,
physician explainer, unboxing and first-night, and the full 21 nights series.
It is also the unit sold as a pair in [[products/sleep-two-pack]] for the
couples lane.

Every product claim made about Sleep in ad copy or on-site must clear
[[decisions/claims-allowlist]] before it ships.
