# Sleep Two-Pack

A two-unit bundle of [[products/helight-sleep]], built for buyers who need one
device per side of the bed rather than one shared unit. It is the anchor SKU
for the couples audience lane in [[wiki/audience-lanes]] and the subject of
[[campaigns/couples-two-pack]].

The two-pack solves a real friction point that a single-unit purchase does
not: a device placed on one nightstand only reaches the partner facing that
direction, and light exposure for a therapy built on ipRGC stimulation
(see [[wiki/study-chain]]) depends on facing the source. Creative for this
SKU should show two units, one per nightstand, rather than a couple sharing
one device, since that is not how the mechanism is meant to be used.

The bundle carries the same 21-nights promise, 60-day guarantee and two-year
warranty as the single unit, per unit. Gifting occasions (anniversaries,
housewarming, a couple starting to cohabit) are the strongest hook angle for
this SKU; see [[writing/hook-vault]] for gifting-specific templates.
