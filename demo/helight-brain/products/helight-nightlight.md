# Helight Nightlight

$25. The smallest and cheapest item in the line, a companion device rather
than a sleep-onset therapy tool. Currently out of stock on the US store, which
[[sops/store-auditor]] flags weekly as a live gap rather than a resolved one:
until restocked, no active campaign should route paid traffic to this
product's page.

Because it is unavailable, the Nightlight is a poor gift-with-purchase or
upsell candidate in any current Klaviyo flow (see
[[sops/lifecycle-planner]]), and any evergreen ad creative referencing "the
full Helight lineup" should be checked against current stock before it airs.

Positioning, when it is back in stock, is a low-commitment add-on for a buyer
who already owns [[products/helight-sleep]] or [[products/helight-kidzzz]] and
wants a dim, non-disruptive light for hallway or nursery use overnight, distinct
from the 28-minute protocol device.

(battery life, brightness levels, dimensions) exists in the public brief and
