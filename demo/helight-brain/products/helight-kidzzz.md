# Helight Kidzzz

$139. Same core mechanism as [[products/helight-sleep]], 630 nm light for 14
minutes with a 14-minute fade, packaged and marketed for children rather than
adults. It is the anchor product for the parents-of-bad-sleepers audience lane
in [[wiki/audience-lanes]] and the subject of [[campaigns/parents-kidzzz]].

Kidzzz carries the same 21-nights-or-less promise, 60-night guarantee and
two-year warranty as the adult product, and is covered by the same patent
family. The product page does not, and should not, claim any pediatric
clinical study beyond what is in [[wiki/study-chain]]; none of the six
published studies were run on children, and no page should imply otherwise.

The safe-for-infants angle in [[wiki/angle-bank]] is a positioning angle, not a
medical claim, and copy referencing it must be reviewed by
[[sops/compliance-auditor]] before it runs. Creative for this SKU favours the
parent-POV format from [[wiki/format-playbook]], shot from the perspective of
a parent watching a child settle rather than a product-only shot.
