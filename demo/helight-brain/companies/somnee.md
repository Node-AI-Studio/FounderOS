# Somnee

Sleep headband built on neurostimulation, marketed as a personalised,
app-guided device that adapts its signal to the wearer's brain activity over
repeated use. Premium price point, worn nightly, requires an app and an
account to function and to track "personalisation" progress over time.

Somnee is the sharpest contrast case in the bedside-competitor set. It is a
wearable, it is app-gated, it requires an account, and its mechanism (EEG-based
neurostimulation) is presented as more technologically sophisticated than
light exposure, which cuts against Helight on the "high-tech" axis but for
Helight on the "simple and provable" axis.

The load-bearing angle here is [[wiki/angle-bank]]'s "no app no account no
subscription" line, plus "it does nothing except turn off": Helight's entire
interaction model is pressing one button. Somnee's requires ongoing app use to
get value, which is a real behavioural cost for a tired person at 11pm.

Do not claim Somnee is ineffective or unsafe; the comparison stands on
interaction cost and evidence transparency, not on disparaging a competitor's
mechanism. See [[decisions/claims-allowlist]] and [[wiki/competitor-frame]].
