# Helight

DTC red light therapy brand selling on Shopify direct to consumer, with a
separate Canadian store and a wholesale programme. Storefront runs EN, FR and
DE locales and ships to 40+ countries.

Founded by two families: the Granges in France, who met a photobiomodulation
researcher in 2015, and the Bonniers in Canada, who joined in 2016. The two
opened a photobiomodulation centre in Montreal in 2017 and built the
professional device Helight Pro before Helight Sleep launched in Quebec in
October 2020. About 200,000 units have sold since.

A single broadcast report in Quebec calling the underlying science unfounded
nearly ended the company: 40 points of sale lost, revenue cut by two thirds.
See [[wiki/the-quebec-broadcast]] for the full account. The business rebuilt in
the United States, where it now runs under a beauty and wellness operator with
four decades of experience, and picked up retail distribution at Ulta Beauty
(21 October 2025) and Goop.

The product line is three SKUs: [[products/helight-sleep]], [[products/helight-kidzzz]],
and [[products/helight-nightlight]], plus [[products/sleep-two-pack]]. All
built on [[products/the-28-minute-protocol]]. The offer stands on a
21-nights-or-less results promise, a 60-day money-back guarantee, and a
two-year warranty, backed by a granted patent family (see [[wiki/patent-family]]).

Operator: Yannick Kiefer. Seven pillars run the weekly loop: Growth, Content,
Retention, Store, Customer Care, Finance, Operations. See
[[decisions/one-lane-dtc-first]] for the current channel bet and
[[wiki/evidence-base]] for the science the brand leans on.
