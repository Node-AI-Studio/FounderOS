# Ulta Beauty

National beauty retailer. Helight Sleep has been on Ulta shelves since 21
October 2025, the brand's first major beauty-retail placement and a signal
that Helight is being merchandised as a sleep-and-wellness beauty adjacency,
not only a health device.

The relationship is retail, not systems: there is no live data connection
between Ulta's point of sale and any Helight-side tool. Sell-through numbers
arrive by report on whatever cadence Ulta's category team provides, not
through an API or dashboard pull. See [[decisions/retail-is-status-only]] for
why no pillar treats Ulta as a source of live data, and why no agent is built
assuming otherwise.

What Ulta placement does change: DTC creative that references "also at Ulta"
adds a trust signal for a shopper who has never heard of Helight, and the
Store pillar keeps [[sops/store-auditor]] aware of retail-driven traffic spikes
around Ulta's own marketing moments so the site doesn't look under-stocked or
stale by comparison.
