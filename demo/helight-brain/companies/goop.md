# Goop

Gwyneth Paltrow's wellness commerce and editorial platform. Goop carries
Helight as part of its curated sleep and recovery selection, a placement that
functions as much as editorial credibility as it does a sales channel: being
selected by Goop's buying team reads to a certain audience as a quality
signal independent of unit volume.

As with [[companies/ulta-beauty]], the relationship is retail only. There is no
system integration, no live inventory or sales feed, and no agent should be
built assuming one exists. Sell-through and any editorial placement (a
product mention, a gift-guide inclusion) arrive by report, checked manually by
[[people/store-manager]] and folded into the Store pillar's view of the month
rather than treated as a channel with its own dashboard.

Goop's audience skews toward the menopause and students-adjacent wellness
reader more than the shift-worker or traveller lanes in
[[wiki/audience-lanes]], which matters when deciding what proof point or
quote to lead with in any content that name-checks the retailer.
