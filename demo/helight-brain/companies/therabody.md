# Therabody

Recovery and wellness hardware company, known first for percussive massage
guns, now extended into sleep with a pair of SmartGoggles: a worn eye mask
combining heat, gentle compression and vibration to encourage relaxation
before sleep.

The Therabody comparison is a wearable-versus-ambient argument. SmartGoggles
sit on the face for the whole session; Helight Sleep sits on the nightstand
and asks nothing of the user beyond looking toward it. For a buyer who has
already tried and abandoned a sleep mask, weighted blanket or wearable, "you
don't wear this" is a real objection-handler, tracked in
[[wiki/angle-bank]] as the drug-free, no-wearable framing.

Therabody's brand strength, built on the massage-gun line, is real and worth
naming honestly rather than dismissing: it reaches an athletic-recovery
audience that overlaps with Helight's wearable-optimisers lane in
[[wiki/audience-lanes]] and the [[campaigns/wearable-optimisers]] test.

The debunk-format video in [[wiki/format-playbook]] is the natural home for a
direct comparison against a worn device, provided every claim clears
[[decisions/claims-allowlist]] first.
