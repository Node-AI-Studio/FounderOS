# Glamour Beauty Awards 2024

## What it says

Recognised Helight in Glamour's beauty awards for 2024, positioning the
product within a beauty-and-wellness frame rather than purely a medical or
sleep-health frame, consistent with the [[companies/ulta-beauty]] and
[[companies/goop]] retail placements.

## Date

2024.

## Which claims it supports

Supports beauty-and-wellness-framed creative and any copy connecting the
product to a self-care routine rather than a clinical one. Indexed in
[[wiki/awards-and-press]].

[[sops/ad-copywriter]] pulls this citation specifically for
[[campaigns/couples-two-pack]] creative, where a beauty-and-self-care frame
outperforms a purely clinical one.

[[people/retention-lead]] has asked whether this citation belongs in
[[writing/email-voice]]'s post-purchase sequence as a light reassurance line,
a question still open as of the most recent [[sops/approve-the-week]] review.

associated interview or feature article, only the award listing itself.
