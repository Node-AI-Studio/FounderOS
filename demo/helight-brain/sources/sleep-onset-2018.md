# Sleep Onset Study (2018)

## What it says

Studied the effect of the light exposure mechanism specifically on sleep
onset, the time it takes to fall asleep, rather than on circadian timing or
performance more broadly. The first study in the chain to speak directly to
the outcome Helight Sleep is sold on.

## Date

2018.

## Which claims it supports

The core evidentiary basis for any claim about falling asleep faster, subject
always to [[wiki/claims-allowlist]] phrasing limits. Central to
[[wiki/study-chain]] and cited in comparison content against melatonin in
[[campaigns/melatonin-quitters]].

[[sops/ad-copywriter]] treats this as the single most load-bearing study for
day-to-day ad copy, since it speaks most directly to the outcome most buyers
are actually shopping for.

[[people/head-of-growth]] treats any new melatonin-comparison creative as
incomplete without a direct citation back to this study, a standard
[[sops/ad-copywriter]] now applies by default.
