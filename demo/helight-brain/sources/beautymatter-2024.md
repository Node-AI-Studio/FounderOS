# BeautyMatter Next 2024

## What it says

Named Helight to BeautyMatter's Next list in 2024, an industry-trade
recognition aimed at brands the beauty industry itself considers rising,
distinct from a consumer-facing lifestyle award.

## Date

2024.

## Which claims it supports

Most useful in wholesale and retail-facing materials rather than consumer ad
copy, since a trade recognition signals credibility to a buyer or retail
partner more than to an end consumer. Indexed in [[wiki/awards-and-press]].

Alongside [[sources/fast-company-2026]] and [[sources/oprah-daily-2025]], this
award rounds out the four-award set referenced together in
[[wiki/evidence-base]].

[[people/store-manager]] noted this recognition specifically targets
wholesale and retail buyers rather than end consumers, which shapes where it
eventually appears once [[projects/fill-the-proof-pages]] closes out the
proof-page gap.

No new BeautyMatter recognition is expected before the next award cycle, so
this page's status remains stable rather than pending an update.
