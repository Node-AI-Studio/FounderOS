# Patent Family Record

## What it says

The complete filing record for the patent family covering Helight's 28-minute
light-and-fade protocol, granted or filed across seven jurisdictions: US
11672943 B2, EP 3519030 A1, JP 7277925 B2, CN 109982736 A, FR 3056408 A1, ES
2928405 T3, and WO 2018060616 A1 (the PCT/WIPO filing that anchors the
family).

## Date

Filings span multiple years under a single WIPO priority record; treat the
family as current and granted as of 2026 rather than citing a single filing
date.

## Which claims it supports

The primary source behind [[wiki/patent-family]] and claim 5 in
[[wiki/claims-allowlist]]. Any ad or page citing a specific patent number
should pull it directly from this record rather than from a secondary
summary, to avoid transcription drift across jurisdictions.
