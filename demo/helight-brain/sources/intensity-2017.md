# Intensity Study (2017)

## What it says

Investigated how light intensity affects the strength of the circadian
signalling effect established in [[sources/berson-hattar-2002]], narrowing
the applied question from "does light exposure timing matter" toward "how
much light, and at what strength, produces a reliable effect."

## Date

2017.

wavelength and not just any red light."
