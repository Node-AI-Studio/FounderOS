# Basketball Players Study (2012)

## What it says

Examined light exposure timing and its relationship to sleep quality and
next-day performance outcomes in a group of basketball players, an early
applied test of the ipRGC-based mechanism established in
[[sources/berson-hattar-2002]] in an athletic population.

## Date

2012.

## Which claims it supports

outcomes. Supports the wearable-optimisers audience lane in
[[wiki/audience-lanes]] and [[campaigns/wearable-optimisers]], since an
athletic-performance angle resonates with a buyer already tracking recovery
metrics on a wearable. Referenced in [[wiki/evidence-base]].
