# Sleep Inertia Study (2019)

## What it says

Examined sleep inertia, the grogginess and impaired alertness on waking,
and its relationship to the light-exposure mechanism, extending the study
chain from sleep onset into waking-side outcomes.

## Date

2019.

## Which claims it supports

Supports any content addressing morning grogginess or waking quality, an
under-used angle relative to the sleep-onset framing that dominates current
copy. Part of [[wiki/study-chain]] and a candidate source for content aimed
at the shift-workers and nurses lane in [[wiki/audience-lanes]], where
waking quality after an irregular sleep schedule is often a bigger pain
point than falling asleep itself.

[[people/dr-ed-omalley]] has referenced this study directly in past public
commentary, making it a natural citation to pair with his voice in the
physician-explainer format.
