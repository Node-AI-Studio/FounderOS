# Dr Ed O'Malley

Physician who has publicly endorsed Helight. His public commentary leans
toward the mechanism itself: framing the 28-minute protocol
([[products/the-28-minute-protocol]]) as consistent with known circadian
science rather than presenting it as a breakthrough beyond what the published
research supports, which makes him the most conservative and most
defensible of the three endorsing voices to quote directly.

Cited candidate for the shift-workers and nurses audience lane in
[[wiki/audience-lanes]], where a credentialed, science-forward tone tends to
outperform a lifestyle-forward one, and a natural voice for the debunk-format
video (see [[wiki/format-playbook]]) that addresses blue-light-glasses
alternatives.

As with the other two endorsing physicians, sign-off through
[[sops/compliance-auditor]] is required before any new use of his commentary,
and the doctor-recommended page gap tracked in
[[wiki/credibility-gaps-on-the-site]] applies to him as well.
