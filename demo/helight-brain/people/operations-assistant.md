# Operations Assistant

Owns the Operations pillar: the connective tissue that keeps every other
pillar's tools, data and documentation in sync. This is the pillar
[[projects/connect-the-stack]] belongs to, since wiring Meta, TikTok, Google,
Klaviyo and Shopify data into one place is infrastructure, not a growth or
content deliverable on its own.

The core agent reporting into this role is [[sops/knowledge-agent]], which
keeps this brain itself current: new sources filed under sources/, new
decisions cross-linked from the sops/ and decisions/ they govern, and stale
pages flagged rather than silently left to rot.

The Operations Assistant is also the practical owner of brand-asset hygiene
(brand pack versioning, approved photography, templates; see media/) even
though no dedicated hero agent in this brief covers that ground yet, which is
itself tracked as an open gap rather than quietly absorbed into someone's
informal side duties.
