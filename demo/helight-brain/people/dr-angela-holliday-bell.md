# Dr Angela Holliday-Bell

Physician and sleep medicine specialist who has publicly endorsed Helight.
Her public position centres on light exposure timing as a legitimate,
underused lever for sleep onset, consistent with the ipRGC research chain in
[[wiki/study-chain]] rather than a novel or unsubstantiated claim.

Cited on [[products/helight-sleep]]'s physician-endorsement copy and in the
physician-explainer video format from [[wiki/format-playbook]]. Any quote
attributed to her must match language she has actually used publicly; the
Content pillar does not draft physician quotes and attribute them
retroactively.

currently rendering empty (see [[wiki/credibility-gaps-on-the-site]]), which
means her endorsement, along with [[people/dr-pedram-navab]]'s and
