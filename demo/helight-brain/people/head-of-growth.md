# Head of Growth

Owns the Growth pillar: paid acquisition across Meta, TikTok and Google.
Reports weekly numbers into [[sops/approve-the-week]] and brings every scaling
request through [[sops/approve-spend-on-winners]], since spend on a winning
angle is Yannick Kiefer's call, not a standing budget the pillar can spend
unilaterally.

Agents reporting into this role: [[sops/growth-planner]] (weekly plan and
allocation across platforms), [[sops/ad-copywriter]] (angle-to-copy drafting),
[[sops/compliance-auditor]] (claims and platform-policy gate before anything
publishes), and [[sops/budget-auditor]] (pacing and marginal-return checks
against the approved budget).

The Head of Growth is the first stop for a proposed new audience lane from
[[wiki/audience-lanes]] before it becomes a live test in campaigns/, and the
last checkpoint before a Compliance Auditor finding gets escalated to Yannick
rather than fixed in copy.

Works most closely with [[people/content-lead]], since the weekly loop runs
Content finding winners and Growth funding them.
