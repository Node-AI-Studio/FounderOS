# Retention Lead

Owns the Retention pillar: everything in Klaviyo, from welcome flows through
post-purchase coaching to win-back. Retention is the third leg of the weekly
loop: Content finds winners, Growth funds them, Retention keeps the buyer once
Growth has paid to bring them in.

Agents reporting into this role: [[sops/lifecycle-planner]] (owns the flow
map and trigger logic across the customer lifecycle), [[sops/21-nights-coach]]
(the post-purchase email and SMS sequence that coaches a buyer through the
21-night usage window so the results promise has a real chance of landing),
and [[sops/offer-designer]] (structures win-back offers, bundle framing and
the guarantee as risk reversal per [[decisions/guarantee-as-risk-reversal]]).

[[projects/seven-klaviyo-flows]] is this pillar's current build: getting all
seven core lifecycle flows live and instrumented. The Retention Lead is also
the primary consumer of [[sops/voice-of-customer]] output, since objection
patterns from Customer Care usually turn into flow copy changes before they
turn into ad copy changes.
