# Store Manager

Owns the Store pillar: the Shopify storefront itself and everything that
counts as on-site proof. That includes the four credibility pages that
currently render empty (science, doctor-recommended, compare, better-sleep;
see [[wiki/credibility-gaps-on-the-site]]), which makes closing that gap the
Store Manager's most overdue open item, tracked in
[[projects/fill-the-proof-pages]].

Agents reporting into this role: [[sops/store-auditor]] (site health, stock
status, and page-level QA across EN/FR/DE and the Canadian store),
[[sops/evidence-curator]] (keeps the science and study content on-site
accurate and sourced against [[wiki/evidence-base]]), and
[[sops/ai-answer-optimizer]] (makes product and proof pages legible to AI
answer engines, feeding [[wiki/ai-answer-share-of-voice]]).

The Store Manager is the natural owner of retailer status checks for
[[companies/ulta-beauty]] and [[companies/goop]], since both are status-only
relationships that live on the storefront side of the business rather than
inside a live data feed. See [[decisions/retail-is-status-only]].
