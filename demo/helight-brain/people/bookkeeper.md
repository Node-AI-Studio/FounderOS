# Bookkeeper

Owns the Finance pillar: the numbers behind every decision the other six
pillars make. Refunds from [[sops/refund-handler]], ad spend from
[[sops/budget-auditor]], and every SKU's landed cost roll up here before
Yannick sees them in [[sops/approve-the-week]].

The core agent reporting into this role is [[sops/contribution-margin]], which
turns raw revenue and spend numbers from every pillar into a per-SKU and
per-lane margin view, since a lane that produces revenue at a loss is not a
lane worth funding regardless of what the top-line trend shows.

The Bookkeeper is the final check before any spend increase from
[[sops/approve-spend-on-winners]] is signed off, because a "winner" by
click-through rate can still be a loser by contribution margin once the
guarantee-driven refund rate for that lane is factored in. See
[[decisions/guarantee-as-risk-reversal]] for why refunds are treated as a cost
of the offer rather than a support-pillar failure.
