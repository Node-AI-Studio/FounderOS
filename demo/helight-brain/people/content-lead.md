# Content Lead

Owns the Content pillar: organic content and creator relationships. The
Content Lead's agents surface what is working before Growth ever spends a
dollar on it, which makes this role the first half of the weekly loop
described in [[sops/approve-the-week]].

Agents reporting into this role: [[sops/content-planner]] (weekly content
calendar across formats), [[sops/creator-watcher]] (tracks creator output and
performance), [[sops/hook-miner]] (surfaces hook candidates from
[[writing/hook-vault]] and live performance data), and [[sops/publisher]]
(the actual posting step, gated by [[sops/compliance-auditor]] first).

A separate Content agent, [[sops/21-nights-producer]], owns the day 1 / day 7
/ day 21 documentary series described in [[wiki/the-21-nights-series]], which
sits between organic content and the Retention pillar's story since it
tracks a single buyer's real experience with the product.

Per [[decisions/editing-stays-human]], no agent in this pillar is authorised
to publish an edited video without a person reviewing the cut first.
