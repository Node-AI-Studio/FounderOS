# Dr Pedram Navab

Physician who has publicly endorsed Helight. His public commentary has
focused on the drug-free positioning: light-based sleep support as an
alternative to sedative reliance, which lines up directly with the
quitting-melatonin audience lane in [[wiki/audience-lanes]] and the
[[campaigns/melatonin-quitters]] test.

Cited in copy and creative that leans on the drug-free angle from
[[wiki/angle-bank]], and a candidate source for the physician-explainer format
in [[wiki/format-playbook]] when that lane needs its own dedicated cut rather
than a shared endorsement reel.

As with the other two endorsing physicians, his commentary is not currently
surfaced on the storefront's doctor-recommended page, since that page renders
empty today (see [[wiki/credibility-gaps-on-the-site]]). Any future use of his
name or likeness in paid creative needs sign-off through
[[sops/compliance-auditor]] before it runs, not after.
