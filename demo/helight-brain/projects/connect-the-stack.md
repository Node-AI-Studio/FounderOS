# Project -- Connect the Stack

## Goal

Wire Meta, TikTok, Google, Klaviyo and Shopify data into one place so [[sops/growth-planner]], [[sops/lifecycle-planner]] and [[sops/contribution-margin]] work from the same numbers instead of five separate exports.

## Status

In progress. Meta and Shopify are connected; TikTok, Google and Klaviyo remain manual pulls as of the most recent [[sops/approve-the-week]] review.

## Next step

Complete the Klaviyo connection next, since [[projects/seven-klaviyo-flows]] depends on it for accurate flow-level attribution.

## Owner pillar

Operations, owned by [[people/operations-assistant]].

[[people/head-of-growth]] and [[people/retention-lead]] are the two pillar
leads most affected by the remaining gaps, since both currently reconcile
numbers manually across platforms until the connection work finishes.

[[people/bookkeeper]] is the last pillar lead to gain a live connection under
this project, since Finance's numbers today are compiled from the other four
pillars' reports rather than pulled directly.
