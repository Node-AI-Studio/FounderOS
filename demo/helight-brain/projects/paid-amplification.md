# Project -- Paid Amplification

## Goal

Scale spend on proven winners across the six active or in-development audience lanes without outrunning [[sops/contribution-margin]]'s view of what is actually profitable.

## Status

In progress. [[campaigns/melatonin-quitters]] and [[campaigns/parents-kidzzz]] are scaling; [[campaigns/shift-workers]] and [[campaigns/travellers]] remain in creative development.

## Next step

Move [[campaigns/shift-workers]] into its first live test week once creative clears [[sops/compliance-auditor]].

## Owner pillar

Growth, owned by [[people/head-of-growth]].

[[sops/contribution-margin]] gates every scale decision here, so a lane
trending well on [[sops/budget-auditor]]'s pacing view still waits for a
margin check before [[sops/approve-spend-on-winners]] considers it.

[[people/content-lead]] supplies the creative this project scales; a lane
cannot move from creative development to a live test without a cleared piece
from [[sops/content-planner]] first.

A sixth lane will not be added until at least one of the current four active
lanes is either fully scaled or paused, keeping the project's scope
deliberately bounded rather than open-ended.
