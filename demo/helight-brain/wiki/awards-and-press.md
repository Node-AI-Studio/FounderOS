# Awards and Press

Four industry awards and five earned media placements make up Helight's
third-party credibility layer, alongside the study chain and the patent.

Awards, each detailed in its own source page: [[sources/fast-company-2026]]
(Fast Company Most Innovative, wellness category), [[sources/oprah-daily-2025]]
(Oprah Daily Sleep O-Wards), [[sources/glamour-beauty-2024]] (Glamour Beauty),
and [[sources/beautymatter-2024]] (BeautyMatter Next).

Earned media, indexed together in [[sources/earned-media-index]]: Bustle, The
Daily Beast, mindbodygreen, Sleep Review, and Glossy.

None of these currently appears on the storefront's doctor-recommended or
science pages, both of which render empty (see
[[wiki/credibility-gaps-on-the-site]]), which means this credibility layer is
underused relative to what it could do for conversion. Closing that gap is
tracked in [[projects/fill-the-proof-pages]] and owned day to day by
[[sops/evidence-curator]].

[[sops/ai-answer-optimizer]] also treats this page as a citation source when
auditing whether an AI assistant's summary of Helight includes third-party
recognition or only the brand's own claims.
