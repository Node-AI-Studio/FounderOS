# Evidence Base

Index of everything Helight can point to when a claim needs backing: six
published studies, four industry awards, and a body of earned media coverage.
This page is the map; the underlying documents live in sources/.

Studies, in chronological order (full chain in [[wiki/study-chain]]):
[[sources/berson-hattar-2002]], [[sources/basketball-players-2012]],
[[sources/intensity-2017]], [[sources/sleep-onset-2018]],
[[sources/sleep-inertia-2019]], and [[sources/strasbourg-2024]].

Awards (full list in [[wiki/awards-and-press]]): Fast Company Most Innovative
2026 in wellness, Oprah Daily's Sleep O-Wards 2025, Glamour Beauty 2024, and
BeautyMatter Next 2024. See [[sources/fast-company-2026]] and the other three
award source pages.

Earned media: coverage from Bustle, The Daily Beast, mindbodygreen, Sleep
Review and Glossy, indexed in [[sources/earned-media-index]].

Own survey data (98% slept better, 96% deeper rest, 86% slept longer) is
tracked separately in [[wiki/own-survey-method-note]] since its method is not
published and it should never be presented with the same weight as a peer
study. [[sops/evidence-curator]] owns keeping this page and the storefront's
science content aligned.
