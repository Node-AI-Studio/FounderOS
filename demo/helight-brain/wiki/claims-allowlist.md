# Claims Allowlist

The actual approved phrasings for Helight's core claims, sourced to the pages
below. [[sops/compliance-auditor]] checks every outbound piece of content
against this list; nothing here is a suggestion.

1. "Red light therapy for sleep, 630 nm, 14 minutes plus a 14-minute fade." --
   [[products/the-28-minute-protocol]].
2. "Sleep better in 21 nights or less, or your money back." -- helight.com hero, see [[wiki/the-21-nights-series]].
3. "60-night money-back trial." -- helight.com product page wording; never "60-day". See [[decisions/guarantee-as-risk-reversal]].
4. "2-year manufacturer's warranty." -- helight.com product page wording.
5. "Backed by a granted patent family." -- [[wiki/patent-family]], cite a
   specific number when space allows.
6. "Grounded in the discovery of intrinsically photosensitive retinal
   ganglion cells." -- [[sources/berson-hattar-2002]].
7. "Supports natural melatonin production." -- helight.com product page wording. "Drug free" is an angle in [[wiki/angle-bank]], not approved copy until Helight signs it off.
8. "No apps, no subscriptions, no setup." -- helight.com product page wording,
   always true, never softened to "minimal app."
9. "In our own survey, 98% reported sleeping better." -- must always carry
   "our own survey," per [[wiki/own-survey-method-note]]. Source: Helight's
   Ulta launch press release, November 2025.
10. "Physician-endorsed." -- only with a named, currently cleared physician
    attached; see [[people/dr-angela-holliday-bell]].
11. Never: any claim of curing insomnia, treating a diagnosed sleep disorder,
    or replacing medical care.
12. Never: an unqualified "safe for infants" without the positioning-only
    framing from [[wiki/angle-bank]].
13. Never: an invented spec not in [[decisions/no-invented-product-specs]].
14. Never: disparaging a named competitor's safety or efficacy; comparisons
    stay on interaction model and evidence transparency, per
    [[wiki/competitor-frame]].
15. Never: a claim exceeding what [[wiki/study-chain]] actually shows, even if
    a stronger version would perform better.
