# Own Survey Method Note

Helight's own customer survey reports 98% slept better, 96% experienced
deeper rest, and 86% slept longer. These are the company's stated figures,
not invented for this brain, but the survey's method (sample size, wording,
self-selection) has not been published. The figures appear in Helight's own
Ulta Beauty launch press release (PRWeb, November 2025), which is the citation
to use when a reviewer asks where the numbers come from.

That distinction matters for how these numbers get used. They belong in
customer-facing proof as "our own survey found," clearly attributed as
self-reported and Helight's own data, never blended into language that implies
independent, peer-reviewed backing the way [[wiki/study-chain]]'s six studies
carry.

[[sops/compliance-auditor]] treats any copy that presents these percentages
without the "our own survey" framing as a compliance issue, not a stylistic
nitpick, since the platform and regulatory risk of an unqualified statistic in
a health-adjacent category is real. See [[wiki/claims-allowlist]] for the
exact approved phrasing pattern for these numbers, and
[[wiki/evidence-base]] for where this sits relative to independently published
research.
