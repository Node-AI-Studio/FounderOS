# Angle Bank

Nine core angles the brand draws on across ad copy, scripts and email, each
gated through [[wiki/claims-allowlist]] before use.

1. NASA origin: the circadian-science lineage behind the mechanism.
2. The granted patent: see [[wiki/patent-family]] for exact numbers to cite.
3. The 28-minute sunset mimic: the mechanical, checkable description of
   [[products/the-28-minute-protocol]].
4. Drug free: positioned against melatonin and sedatives.
5. No app, no account, no subscription: positioned against
   [[companies/hatch]] and [[companies/somnee]].
6. Safe for infants: a positioning name only, never a medical claim; see
   [[decisions/no-invented-product-specs]].
7. Physician endorsement: see [[people/dr-angela-holliday-bell]],
   [[people/dr-pedram-navab]] and [[people/dr-ed-omalley]].
8. The guarantee as risk reversal: see [[decisions/guarantee-as-risk-reversal]].
9. "It does nothing except turn off": the simplicity angle, contrasted
   against app-gated competitors.

Angle-to-lane pairing lives in [[wiki/audience-lanes]]. Every use of angle 6
in particular routes through [[sops/compliance-auditor]] before it ships,
without exception.
