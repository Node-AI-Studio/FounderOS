# Patent Family

Helight's granted patent family covers the 28-minute light-and-fade protocol
described in [[products/the-28-minute-protocol]]. Full filing detail lives in
[[sources/patent-family-record]]; this page is the working summary.

| Jurisdiction | Number |
|---|---|
| United States | US 11672943 B2 |
| Europe | EP 3519030 A1 |
| Japan | JP 7277925 B2 |
| China | CN 109982736 A |
| France | FR 3056408 A1 |
| Spain | ES 2928405 T3 |
| PCT / WIPO | WO 2018060616 A1 |

The patent's existence is one of the strongest angles in [[wiki/angle-bank]]:
"the granted patent" reads as a stronger trust signal to a skeptical buyer
than a general wellness claim, precisely because it is independently
verifiable rather than brand copy. Any ad or page citing the patent should
name at least one specific number rather than saying "patented" alone, since
a checkable specific is what makes the claim land.

and awards as proof infrastructure.
