# Credibility Gaps on the Site

Four pages on the storefront currently render empty: science, doctor-recommended,
compare, and better-sleep. Together they represent the site's entire
dedicated proof-and-comparison surface, and all four are blank.

The science page should hold [[wiki/evidence-base]] in customer-readable
form. The doctor-recommended page should surface
[[people/dr-angela-holliday-bell]], [[people/dr-pedram-navab]] and
[[people/dr-ed-omalley]]. The compare page should hold the honest,
allowlist-cleared comparison against [[companies/hatch]], [[companies/loftie]],
[[companies/therabody]] and [[companies/somnee]] from [[wiki/competitor-frame]].
The better-sleep page has no clear owner yet for what it should even contain.

Beyond the four pages, the blog carries nine near-identical post titles, none
recent, and agents.md is unmodified Shopify boilerplate rather than a
deliberate AI-crawler policy, which undercuts
[[sops/ai-answer-optimizer]]'s work before it starts.

All of this is tracked as one workstream in
[[projects/fill-the-proof-pages]], owned by [[people/store-manager]] and
[[sops/evidence-curator]].
