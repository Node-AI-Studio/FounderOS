# The Quebec Broadcast

A single television broadcast report in Quebec, calling Helight's underlying
science unfounded, nearly ended the company. The brand lost 40 points of
sale and saw revenue cut by two thirds in the aftermath, a scale of damage
from one piece of media coverage that shapes how seriously the company now
treats every claim it makes.

Helight rebuilt in the United States rather than in its original Quebec
market, a pivot that eventually led to the retail placements at
[[companies/ulta-beauty]] and [[companies/goop]] and the current operator
structure under a beauty and wellness veteran with four decades of
experience.

This history is the direct reason [[decisions/compliance-gate-before-publish]]
and [[decisions/claims-allowlist]] exist as hard gates rather than
guidelines: a company that has already been nearly destroyed once by an
unfounded-science narrative treats claims discipline as existential risk
management, not brand hygiene. Every new hire on the Growth or Content pillar
should read this page before touching a claim.
