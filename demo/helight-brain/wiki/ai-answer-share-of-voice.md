# AI Answer Share of Voice

Tracks how often Helight, versus [[companies/hatch]], [[companies/loftie]],
[[companies/therabody]] and [[companies/somnee]], gets cited when an AI
assistant or shopping agent answers a question about red light therapy or
bedside sleep devices.

Owned by [[sops/ai-answer-optimizer]], which audits current citation
frequency, identifies which pages an answer engine can and cannot extract
clean claims from, and proposes fixes. The four empty credibility pages
tracked in [[wiki/credibility-gaps-on-the-site]] are the single biggest
reason Helight currently under-indexes here relative to a competitor with a
populated, structured comparison or science page.

This work is distinct from paid or organic reach: an AI assistant citing
Helight in an answer is a zero-click, high-trust placement that neither
[[sops/growth-planner]] nor [[sops/content-planner]] currently has a lever
for, which is why it is tracked as its own line of work in
[[projects/cited-by-assistants]] rather than folded into either pillar's
existing reporting.
