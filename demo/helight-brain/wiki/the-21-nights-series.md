# The 21 Nights Series

The brand's flagship documentary format, following one real buyer through day
1, day 7 and day 21 with Helight, tracking the "21 nights or less" results
promise honestly rather than only publishing the wins.

Produced by [[sops/21-nights-producer]] on the content side, and mirrored by
[[sops/21-nights-coach]] on the retention side, which runs the actual
four-touch email and SMS sequence a real buyer receives across the same
window, independent of whether that buyer is a documentary participant.

Every cut in the series passes through a human editor per
[[decisions/editing-stays-human]] before [[sops/compliance-auditor]] reviews
it, and a day-21 result that does not support the results promise is still
published rather than discarded, per the escalation note on
[[sops/21-nights-producer]]. That honesty is itself part of the brand's
credibility argument against competitors making broader, less checkable
claims.
