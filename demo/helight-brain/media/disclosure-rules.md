# Disclosure Rules

Working rules for two kinds of disclosure that apply to Helight's creative:
platform AI-generated-content (AIGC) disclosure, and physician-endorsement
disclosure.

Any creative using AI-generated or AI-assisted visuals or voice must carry
the platform's required AIGC disclosure toggle at post time; this sits with
[[sops/publisher]] to confirm before a piece goes live, not left to the
creator's own judgment.

Any content featuring [[people/dr-angela-holliday-bell]],
[[people/dr-pedram-navab]] or [[people/dr-ed-omalley]] must disclose the
nature of their relationship to Helight (paid endorsement, unpaid
commentary, or affiliation) accurately, and that disclosure language is
checked by [[sops/compliance-auditor]] alongside the claims themselves, since
a true claim delivered with a missing or misleading endorsement disclosure is
still a compliance failure.

Related: [[decisions/claims-allowlist]], [[decisions/compliance-gate-before-publish]].

[[sops/ad-copywriter]] and [[sops/content-planner]] are both expected to flag
a disclosure question at draft time rather than leaving it for
[[sops/compliance-auditor]] to catch at the gate, since a late catch delays
the whole week's calendar.
